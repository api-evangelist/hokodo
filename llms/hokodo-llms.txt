# Hokodo

> Hokodo is a B2B payments and embedded trade-credit fintech. It lets merchants and B2B marketplaces offer digital trade accounts and Buy Now, Pay Later (30/60/90-day terms and instalments) at checkout, with Hokodo underwriting the credit risk, financing the invoice, and protecting against non-payment. The BNPL API exposes companies, customers, credit offers, orders and payment plans over a versioned REST interface at https://api.hokodo.co/v1/.
